About me

I am an Information Technology student at Republic Polytechnic, specialising in Full Stack Development. I enjoy turning ideas into websites and exploring how technology and design can make everyday tasks easier.

Beyond my studies, I love clean, simple design and cute cartoon characters. I’m drawn to designs that feel clear and comfortable, with small, playful details that give them personality. This website is a space to bring those interests together and share both my projects and a little of my life.

I’m open to internship and collaboration opportunities where I can contribute, learn from others and grow through hands-on experience. Through school projects and part-time work, I’ve learned to value teamwork, take responsibility and stay patient when challenges arise.

My mission

To keep learning and create useful, thoughtful experiences that bring ease and a little joy to everyday life.

My vision

To become a developer who combines technical skills with creativity, building work that helps people and reflects my personality.
